# Future Me ✦

Write a letter to your future self, choose the day it opens, and keep it sealed until then.

Future Me is a static web app built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It has no frameworks, no build step and no server. Letters are stored only in your browser's `localStorage`.

> **Built with Claude Code.** All of the code in this folder (`index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`) was generated by [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code), Anthropic's AI coding agent, from the prompt below. `CLAUDE.md` was also written by Claude Code, using its `/init` command.

## Features

- **Write and seal letters.** Enter your name, a message and an unlock date. Quick presets set the date for you: 1 minute (demo), 1 week, 1 month, 1 year and 5 years.
- **Countdown dashboard.** Each sealed letter shows a live countdown and a progress bar that fills as its unlock date gets closer.
- **Reveal animation.** When a letter unlocks, its wax seal breaks and the letter unfolds, with a burst of confetti.
- **No peeking.** A sealed letter's text isn't added to the page until it unlocks, so it can't be found by inspecting the page.
- **Filters and stats.** Show all, sealed or opened letters, and see the total, sealed and opened counts and when the next letter opens.
- **Motivational quotes** that rotate over time.
- **Dark and light mode,** with your choice remembered and applied before the page first draws, so it doesn't flash.
- **Responsive** layout that works on mobile.
- **Accessible motion.** Animations are reduced when your system asks for reduced motion.
- **Persistence.** Letters survive a page refresh and stay in sync across browser tabs.

## Running it

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. There's nothing to install.

To see the reveal right away, pick the **1 min demo** preset, seal the letter, and keep the page open until the countdown reaches zero.

## Privacy

Everything stays on your device. Letters are stored in your browser's `localStorage` and are never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them. The only network request the app makes is for Google Fonts.
